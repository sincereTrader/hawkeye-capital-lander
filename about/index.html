Experience

Rajesh has worked through major market chapters — dot-com, Y2K, COVID, and sharp geopolitical shocks. That history informs how Hawkeye talks about risk when headlines get loud.

Rajesh stays conversant with the published letters, interviews, and compounding frameworks associated with investors such as Warren Buffett, Charlie Munger, Peter Lynch, and Raamdeo Agrawal, among others—using that reading as a patient, plain-English lens on risk, ownership, and what long-term investing actually asks of you.

Before Hawkeye, he led sales and marketing at companies including ICI, Mother Dairy, Luminous, Dabur, and Baidyanath — useful practice for explaining money clearly to people who are very good at something other than fund picking.

How Hawkeye works with you

Hawkeye focuses on mutual fund distribution — depth on funds and portfolios, not a kitchen-sink product menu. Rajesh stays in the loop for conversations, portfolio design, and reviews.

Most of us spend our lives working to earn. At Hawkeye Capital, we believe it should be the other way around — your money should work so you don't have to.
